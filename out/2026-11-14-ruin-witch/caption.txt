From a skeleton with a bell to the Trash Witch 💀🔔

Behind the scenes of a Clash of Clans character I worked on. It started in July 2025 as the Artificer, a "shepherd of debris": one of the Witch's kids who summons Constructs from the rubble of destroyed buildings. No magic? No problem, hit the debris hard enough.

It went through a debris hammer, back to the bell, a turnaround and a 3D paintover, then pivoted into a Cleaner before landing as the Rubble Knight and Trash Witch.
